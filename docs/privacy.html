Privacy policy

Last updated 1 November 2026

Asanova (Android package com.asanova.yoga, also on iPhone and iPad) is a guided yoga, breathwork and meditation app. This policy explains what information the app handles, why, who receives it and the choices you have. In short: your answers, body details and practice history stay on your phone; we never send your health answers to anyone; we use PostHog to understand how the app is used and Meta (Facebook) to measure which of our ads bring people to the app; you can switch both off at any time; and payments are handled by Apple or Google. We do not sell your personal information.

1. Who we are

Asanova is developed and published by Pranacodes ("we", "us"). We are the controller of the personal information described here. You can reach us at pranacodes@gmail.com; this address is also our contact point for privacy requests and grievances, including under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023.

Asanova does not have user accounts. You do not need to give us your name, email address or phone number to use it.

2. Information that stays on your device

Your onboarding answers (such as age range, gender, goals, height and weight, activity level, flexibility, balance, experience, areas of the body you want to protect and any physical limitations), your first name if you enter one, your plan, practice history, feelings you log after a session, favourites, downloads, reminders and settings are stored only on your phone or tablet.

We treat answers about pain, injuries, physical limitations and body measurements as health information. They are used on your device to shape your plan and are never sent to us, our analytics provider, Meta or any other company.

You can erase this information at any time in Profile → Reset all data, or by uninstalling the app.

3. Usage statistics (PostHog)

We use PostHog (PostHog Inc., United States) to understand which parts of Asanova help people and where they get stuck. The app sends screen views and named app events, for example: the app was opened and how long it took to start; which onboarding steps were viewed or completed and non-health choices such as goals, experience or preferred session length; paywall views, plan selections, purchases and restores; courses, lessons, meditations and breathing sessions started, finished or left early; lesson download results and times; ratings you give in the app; and settings you change.

These events carry a random identifier created by the app, your app version, language, device type, operating system and approximate country derived from your IP address. They never include your health answers, body measurements, name, email, the text of your feedback or your precise location. There is no session recording, no screen capture and no tracking of what you type.

4. Advertising measurement (Meta)

Asanova shows no ads inside the app. We do advertise Asanova on Facebook and Instagram, and we use the Meta app-events SDK and Meta's Conversions API (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd. and Meta Platforms, Inc.) to measure whether those ads lead to installs and subscriptions, and to show our ads to people more likely to find the app useful.

When this is switched on, Meta receives: that the app was installed and opened; that onboarding was completed; that the plans screen was viewed or a purchase was started; and, through RevenueCat, that a free trial started or a subscription was bought (with its product, price and currency). Together with these events Meta receives device information (such as device model, operating system, app version, language, time zone and IP address), an anonymous app identifier created by the Meta SDK, a scrambled (hashed) version of our anonymous purchase identifier and, where your device allows it, your device's advertising identifier.

We never send Meta your health answers, body measurements, name, email, practice details, meditation or course content, or feedback. Meta processes this information under its own terms and data policy (facebook.com/privacy/policy) and may combine it with information it already holds about you, for example to attribute an install to an ad you saw.

On iPhone and iPad, the app asks your permission (App Tracking Transparency) before your advertising identifier can be used; if you choose Ask App Not to Track, the advertising identifier is not collected and Meta receives only limited, non-tracking measurement. On Android you can reset or delete your advertising identifier in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Google → Ads).

5. Your controls

Profile → Privacy → Usage statistics switches off both PostHog usage statistics and Meta advertising measurement from the app. The choice is stored on your phone and applies immediately. Purchases made after you switch it off are not reported to Meta.

You can also limit tracking in your device settings (iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking; Android: delete or reset your advertising ID), reset all app data in Profile, or uninstall the app. Meta also offers ad preference controls in your Facebook and Instagram settings.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own terms. We never see or store your card or payment details.

We use RevenueCat (RevenueCat, Inc., United States) to check whether you have Asanova Plus and to restore purchases. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user identifier, your store purchase receipts (product, price, currency, dates, renewal and trial status, country), your device type, app version, the anonymous PostHog and Meta app identifiers and, where your device allows it, your advertising identifier, so that a trial or subscription can be counted correctly in our statistics and advertising measurement.

7. Messages you choose to send

When you use Tell us what you need, we store your message, an optional reply email, the submission time, app version and platform in a private inbox hosted by Lovable Cloud. Other app users cannot read these messages. No onboarding answers, practice history, purchase identifier or advertising identifier is attached. Please do not include health or payment details.

We use these messages only to reply and to improve the app, never for advertising.

8. Lesson downloads, reminders and app updates

Lesson films, coaching voice and lesson updates are downloaded from our media server operated by Cloudflare, Inc. Like any web server, it briefly processes your IP address and basic request details to deliver the files and protect the service; we do not use these logs to identify you.

Practice reminders are scheduled locally on your phone; we use no push-notification service and no notification token.

9. Why we use information (legal bases)

We use information to provide the app and the subscription you bought (performance of a contract); to keep the app working, secure and improving and to understand usage (our legitimate interests in running and improving Asanova, balanced against your rights and the controls described above); to measure and improve our advertising (our legitimate interest in growing Asanova, or your consent where the law requires consent, including your App Tracking Transparency choice on iOS); and to meet legal, tax and accounting obligations.

Health information you enter is processed only on your own device, at your request, to personalise your plan; it is not processed by us.

10. Who receives information

We share information only with the providers named in this policy, each for the purpose described: Apple and Google (payments and app distribution), RevenueCat (subscription status), PostHog (usage statistics), Meta (advertising measurement), Cloudflare (media delivery) and Lovable Cloud (the feedback inbox). We may also disclose information if required by law, to protect our rights or users' safety, or as part of a merger or sale of the business under this policy's protections.

We do not sell personal information for money. Under some US state laws (such as California's), sharing device identifiers and app events with Meta for advertising measurement may be considered "sharing" or "targeted advertising"; you can opt out of it at any time with the Usage statistics switch or your device's advertising settings, or by emailing us.

11. International transfers

Our providers may process information in the United States, the European Union, India and other countries. Where required, transfers rely on safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses or the EU–US Data Privacy Framework, and on the providers' contractual commitments.

12. How long information is kept

On-device data stays until you reset it or uninstall the app. Usage statistics are kept for up to 24 months and then deleted or aggregated. Meta keeps advertising data according to its own policy. Purchase records are kept by Apple, Google and RevenueCat as required for tax and accounting. Feedback messages are kept only as long as needed to handle your request and related follow-up.

13. Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example the EU or UK under the GDPR, India under the DPDP Act, Brazil under the LGPD, or California and other US states), you may have the right to access, correct, delete or receive a copy of your personal information, to object to or restrict its use, to withdraw consent, to opt out of targeted advertising, and to nominate someone to act for you. We will not treat you differently for using these rights.

Because Asanova has no accounts, most information is linked only to random identifiers, so we may not be able to match it to you. To make a request, email pranacodes@gmail.com. The quickest way to stop usage statistics and advertising measurement is the Usage statistics switch, and Reset all data starts a new random identifier. We answer within the time the law requires (usually 30 days). You may also complain to your local data protection authority, or in India to the Data Protection Board of India.

14. Security

Information sent by the app travels over encrypted (HTTPS) connections, provider access is limited to what each service needs, and we keep no copy of your health answers anywhere. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, so please keep your device protected with a passcode.

15. Children

Asanova is made for adults. It is not directed to children under 16 (or the age of digital consent where you live), and we do not knowingly collect information from them. If you believe a child has used the app and information was shared, contact us and we will delete what we can.

16. Health notice

Asanova is a general wellness app, not a medical device and not a healthcare provider, and nothing in it is medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. Please read the Health and safety section of our Terms of use and talk to your doctor before starting, especially if you are pregnant or live with any medical condition.

17. Changes and contact

If we change what is collected or shared, we will update this policy, change the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app.

Questions, requests or complaints: pranacodes@gmail.com.

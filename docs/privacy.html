Privacy policy

Last updated 23 October 2026

Asanova is a guided yoga, breathwork and meditation app. This policy explains what happens to your information when you use it. The short version: your answers, body details and practice history stay on your phone, the app contains no advertising or tracking tools, and purchases are handled by Apple or Google.

Information that stays on your device

Your onboarding answers (such as age range, goals, body measurements, activity level, flexibility and balance, and any areas you want to protect), your plan, practice history, favourites, reminders and settings are stored only on your phone. Asanova has no accounts and does not upload this information.

You can erase it at any time in Profile → Reset all data, or by uninstalling the app.

Messages you choose to send

When you use Tell us what you need, we store your message, optional reply email, submission time, app version and platform in a private inbox hosted by Lovable Cloud. Other app users cannot read these messages. No onboarding answers, practice history, purchase identifier or advertising identifier is attached. Please do not include health or payment details.

We use these messages to respond to requests and improve the app, not for advertising. Our hosting provider also processes technical connection information to deliver the request. To request access or deletion, email pranacodes@gmail.com and identify your message.

We retain messages only as long as needed to handle the request and related follow-up. Resetting the app does not delete a message you have already sent.

Purchases and subscriptions

Payments are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play under their own terms. We never see your card or payment details. Your Asanova Plus status is kept on your phone.

No advertising or tracking

Asanova contains no advertising SDKs, analytics trackers or ad-measurement tools. We do not collect your advertising identifier and we do not share your activity with advertisers or data brokers.

Notifications, audio and video

Practice reminders are scheduled locally on your phone; no push service or notification token is used. Coaching voice, music and films are bundled with the app and play offline.

Your rights

Because your practice data never leaves your phone, you control it directly. For a message you sent us, you can ask us to access or delete it by emailing us. You may also complain to your local data protection authority.

Retention and security

On-device data stays until you reset it or uninstall the app. Purchase records are kept by Apple or Google as required for tax and accounting. Messages you send travel over encrypted connections and may be processed outside your country under our provider's safeguards.

Children

Asanova is made for adults. It is not directed to children under 16, and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes and contact

If we change what is collected or shared, we will update this policy and tell you in the app.

Questions or requests: pranacodes@gmail.com.

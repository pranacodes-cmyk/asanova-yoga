1. Health and safety — please read first

Asanova provides general wellness and educational guidance on yoga postures, breathing and meditation. It is not a medical device, it is not provided by doctors, physiotherapists or other licensed healthcare professionals, and nothing in the app is medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. It does not replace care from a qualified professional. Do not ignore or delay professional advice because of something in the app.

Ask your doctor before starting Asanova or any new exercise, breathing or meditation practice — and in particular if you are pregnant, may be pregnant or have recently given birth; are recovering from surgery, an injury or an illness; or live with a heart or circulation condition, high or low blood pressure, glaucoma or other eye conditions, osteoporosis, a back, neck or joint problem, a hernia, diabetes, epilepsy or seizures, asthma or other breathing conditions, dizziness or balance problems, or any other medical condition.

Some practices are not right for everyone. Avoid inversions, deep twists and strong backbends unless your doctor agrees and you feel ready. Breath retention (holding the breath), fast or forceful breathing and abdominal techniques should be skipped if you are pregnant, or have high blood pressure, heart disease, epilepsy, a history of panic attacks, recent abdominal surgery or a hernia. Never practise breathing exercises in water, while driving or while operating machinery.

Meditation and deep relaxation can sometimes bring up strong emotions or memories. If you live with a mental health condition, or feel distressed during or after practice, pause and speak to a qualified professional. Asanova is not a crisis service: if you are in crisis or thinking about harming yourself, contact your local emergency number or a crisis helpline straight away.

Listen to your body. Move only within a comfortable, pain-free range, use a chair, wall, blanket or cushion whenever you need support, practise on a stable non-slip surface with enough space around you, and stop immediately if you feel pain, dizziness, faintness, chest pain or pressure, shortness of breath, numbness or unusual discomfort. If symptoms do not settle quickly, seek medical help — in an emergency, call your local emergency number.

Our cues, timings and modifications are general and cannot take your individual body, health or history into account, even when your plan is personalised from your answers. Your plan is a suggestion, not a prescription. Results differ from person to person and we make no promise of any particular outcome, such as weight loss, pain relief or the cure or prevention of any condition.

Descriptions of yogic traditions — such as chakras, energy, mantras or mudras — are shared for cultural and contemplative context. They are not medical or scientific claims.

You choose whether, when and how to practise, and you do so voluntarily and at your own risk. Children under 16 should not use the app.

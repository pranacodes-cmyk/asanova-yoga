Health first

Asanova offers general wellness guidance, not medical advice. Talk to your doctor before starting any new exercise programme, especially if you are pregnant, recovering from surgery or injury, or live with a heart, joint, balance, blood-pressure or other medical condition.

Move within a comfortable range, use a chair, wall, blanket or cushion for support whenever you need it, skip inversions and strong breathing techniques if they are not right for you, and stop immediately if you feel pain, dizziness, shortness of breath or discomfort.

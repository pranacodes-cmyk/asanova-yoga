Need help with Asanova? Email us and we usually reply within one working day. Tell us your phone model and what happened.

Subscriptions

Restore a purchase on a new phone with Profile → Asanova Plus → Restore purchases, or the Restore link on the plans screen.

Cancel or change a subscription in your App Store account (iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or in Google Play (Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions). Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

India UPI, when available: revoke the Asanova Autopay mandate in your UPI app to stop future debits. For cancellation or restoration assistance, contact Mohit at Prana Codes; we acknowledge requests within 2 working days and verify payment ownership. Payments are non-refundable except verified non-delivery of service (Plus did not unlock and could not be restored) — contact us within 30 days with the UTR for a refund in 5–7 working days after verification. Mandatory consumer rights remain unaffected. Never share your UPI PIN or OTP.

Practice safely

Asanova is general wellness guidance, not medical advice. Check with your doctor before starting, especially if you are pregnant or live with a medical condition. Every course offers gentler options, and chair and wall classes are there whenever you want support. Stop if anything hurts, or if you feel dizzy or short of breath. In an emergency, call your local emergency number.

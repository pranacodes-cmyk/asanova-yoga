Support

Last updated 23 October 2026

Need help with Asanova? Email us and we usually reply within one working day. Tell us your phone model and what happened.

Subscriptions

Restore a purchase on a new phone with Profile → Asanova Plus → Restore purchases, or the Restore link on the plans screen.

Cancel or change a subscription in your App Store account (iPhone: Settings → your name → Subscriptions) or in Google Play (Play Store → profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions). Refunds are handled by Apple or Google.

Practice safely

Every course offers gentler options, and chair and wall classes are there whenever you want support. Stop if anything hurts and check with your doctor before starting if you have a medical condition.
